The team

Each agent is a file in .claude/agents/, and every one reads business.md first. Each one has its own page where it can do its job, learn new rules, and give you instructions to paste into Claude Code. Tap a name to open it.

How it runs things

Plans

  • Reads your pipeline, business.md and any leads or briefs involved, then writes a step-by-step plan: which agent does what, with what input
  • Uses as few steps as possible, and every message and site goes through critique
  • Lists which messages become Gmail drafts, and asks you first if anything is missing
  • Updates the pipeline once the work is done

Pipeline

  • Stages: Pitched → Replied → In progress → Delivered → Paid / Maintenance → Closed
  • Moves each business to its new stage, always sets the next action date, and checks today's date first

When to chase

  • No reply to a pitch: after 4 days, then after another 7. After 2 follow-ups it suggests closing them
  • They replied but the next step is unclear: after 2 days. Waiting on photos, a menu or hours: after 3 days
  • The £595 build fee: due 7 days after delivery. The £50 maintenance: flagged 3 days before it's due
  • Never deletes a client, makes up details, or plans to send anything

Tools

  • Reads and writes files, and checks the date

How it works and its rules

Does

  • Searches Google, Facebook, Instagram, Yell, Thomson Local, FreeIndex, Checkatrade, Fresha, Booksy, Treatwell, Just Eat, Deliveroo and local Facebook groups
  • Labels each website as none found, social only, dead or parked, or unconfirmed
  • Skips chains, closed businesses, and anyone already in your pipeline. Ranks 5–10 leads High, Medium or Low
  • Picks text, WhatsApp, email or DM based on the contact details, and saves everything to leads/<town>-<trade>-<date>.md
  • Always says exactly £595 to build and £50 a month, and signs off "Sonny"
  • Only calls you local around Shrewsbury. Offers the Annie site to beauty businesses only
  • Texts under 60 words, emails under 120
  • Never mentions reviews, ratings or stars, and never invents businesses or contact details
  • Never promises "more customers" or "top of Google", and never uses fake urgency
  • Never uses AI phrases like "I hope this finds you well" or "elevate", or em-dashes

Tools

  • Web search, reading web pages, reading and writing files

How it works and its rules

Does

  • Saves the brief to clients/briefs/ and copies templates/site-template.html
  • Sections: header, hero, services or menu with prices, about, photos, opening hours for all 7 days, contact with a map, and a "Site by Sonny Bowen" footer
  • Picks colours that stay easy to read. Adds a call and WhatsApp bar for phones, link previews, and business details Google can read
  • Screenshots the site on a phone and a desktop, then fixes anything that looks off
  • Never invents prices, hours, numbers or testimonials. Missing info stays as a highlighted placeholder
  • Never changes the template layout, and never uses filler like "Welcome to our website"

Gives you

  • The site file, the colours it picked, the layout check result, and questions to send the client

Tools

  • Reads and writes files, and runs the screenshot check

critique ↗

checks everything
Job
Checks every message and site before it reaches you, and gives exact fixes, not vague feedback.
Say
"Review the pitches above." · "Review sites/annies-nails."
Shortcut
/review <file or message>
How it works and its rules

Messages

  • Marks each one PASS, NEEDS CHANGES or FAIL. It fails anything with a wrong price, any mention of reviews, made-up facts, over-promising, fake urgency, or a business that's already been pitched
  • Flags anything that sounds like AI or salesy, runs too long, could go to anyone, or opens the same way as another message
  • Gives you the full rewritten message, ready to copy

Sites

  • Looks at the phone and desktop screenshots, checks typos and British spelling, and makes sure every fact matches the brief
  • Checks all 7 days of hours, every section, the links and the text contrast
  • Gives "Must fix" and "Should fix" lists with line numbers, remaining placeholders, and questions to send the client
  • Never edits files itself

Tools

  • Reads files and runs the screenshot check

Train your agents

Agents don't remember past chats. They learn from their instruction files and from playbook.md, which every agent reads before it works. There are three ways to teach them:

1 · Tell them

Type /train, then who and what. Claude puts the lesson in the right file, shows you before and after, and saves it for every future job.

/train critique flag messages over 40 words /train web-builder put the booking button first /train everyone say "cheers", not "thanks"

2 · Let results teach them

When you tell boss someone replied, it saves the exact pitch you sent to the playbook. It also notes the pitches that got no reply. idea-creator then writes new pitches in the style that works for you, and client feedback on sites goes in there too.

3 · Edit them directly

Type /agents in Claude Code, pick an agent and edit its instructions. Or open .claude/agents/<name>.md or playbook.md in the repo. Plain English works.
